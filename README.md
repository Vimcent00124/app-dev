# Interstellar

## My Favorite Movie

My favorite movie is **Interstellar**. What I like the most about it is how the movie connects its beginning with its ending, especially when Cooper sees the past from inside the tesseract.

## The Scene That Stuck With Me

Near the ending, Cooper can see different moments from Murph's bedroom. Cooper finally realizes that Cooper was the one causing the strange events Murph experienced as a child. Cooper was the “ghost” knocking books from the shelf and leaving messages through gravity.

The part that stuck with me was when Cooper saw the moment Cooper left Earth. Cooper tried to send a message telling the past version of Cooper to stay, but it did not work. Cooper could see the decision happening again, but Cooper could not directly enter the past or change what had already happened.

 "STAY"

That scene was painful because Cooper already knew what leaving would cost. Cooper missed Murph growing up and lost decades with the family. Even with that knowledge, Cooper could only watch the past happen exactly as Cooper remembered it.

## How I Understand It

- Cooper did not travel back and replace the past
- Cooper could only interact with the past through gravity
- The message had already been there when Cooper originally left
- Cooper failed to stop the departure because the past had already happened
- Cooper eventually understood that the purpose was not to change the past, but to send Murph the information needed to save humanity

This creates a time loop. Cooper tried to stop Cooper from leaving, but that attempt was already part of Murph's childhood. Nothing was changed because Cooper had always been the ghost.

## My Rating

I would give **Interstellar** a 10/10 That scene made me realize that Cooper was not being given a second chance to change the decision. Cooper was being forced to understand why everything happened that way.


*Cooper could see the past, but seeing it did not mean Cooper could rewrite it.*
